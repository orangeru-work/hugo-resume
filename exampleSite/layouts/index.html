Key Features

  • Separation of Data & Presentation: Keep your resume details structured in YAML and manage styling independently.
  • Multiple Built-In Styles: Choose between default and stark themes or supply custom CSS overrides.
  • Print & PDF Optimized: Dedicated print stylesheets with page-break rules preventing orphan section headers and broken entry blocks.
  • ATS Friendly: Clean, semantic HTML structure designed for high Applicant Tracking System compatibility.
